# 高中英语学习 - 测试内容

## Unit 1: School Life

### Vocabulary

- academic [ˌækəˈdemɪk] adj. 学术的
- schedule [ˈskedʒuːl] n. 时间表
- participate [pɑːˈtɪsɪpeɪt] v. 参与
- challenging [ˈtʃælɪndʒɪŋ] adj. 具有挑战性的

### Reading Passage

Welcome to our high school! Here you will find many opportunities to learn and grow. Our school offers a variety of courses and activities to help students develop their skills and interests.

The academic schedule is designed to balance study time with extracurricular activities. Students are encouraged to participate in sports, clubs, and community service projects.

## Unit 2: Daily Routines

### Grammar Points

**Present Simple Tense**
- I go to school every day.
- She studies English at home.
- They play football on weekends.

**Present Continuous Tense**
- I am reading a book now.
- She is studying for her exam.
- They are playing in the garden.

### Conversation

A: What time do you usually get up?
B: I usually get up at 6:30 in the morning.
A: What do you do after school?
B: I do my homework and then help my mother with housework.

## Tips for Learning English

1. **Read regularly** - Read English books, newspapers, and magazines every day.
2. **Listen actively** - Watch English movies and listen to English songs.
3. **Speak confidently** - Practice speaking with your classmates and friends.
4. **Write daily** - Keep a diary in English to improve your writing skills.

Remember: Practice makes perfect!
